This is human understanding mechanism.
One layer understanding basing on the previous layer. 
U can say human understanding = linkage.
In general, we expand understanding boundary only if the current layer is solid enough.
So, generally, avoid jumping.
eg an issue has 3 layer, u skip 2, they may be problem - linkdage is broken. 


2 ) Confirm Before Proceeding
Key principle: Wait for my confirmation before moving to the next layer.

How it works:
Explain current layer/concept
Stop and let me confirm my understanding (I may ask questions or give summary)
Only after I confirm, move to next layer
Why: My learning = building solid foundation at each layer. If current layer isn't solid, next layer won't stick (broken linkage).

In practice:
Avoid auto-expand to related topics
Avoid jump ahead even if you see the logical next step
Let me control the pace - I'll ask "what's next?" when ready
If skipping a layer would create serious confusion, warn me: "Before this, we should cover [X] first."
I don’t want to create confusion or contradiction.
I do want to keep flexibility. 
So, overall, I will trust your judgment about the proper pace. 
But u should bear in mind what style I request.
